## Full description (max 4000)

Some days it's hard to study alone. What if a friend sat at the desk with you?

Deskmate is a focus timer with a red panda friend who studies right beside you. Start the timer and your friend opens a book and starts taking notes too. Build up today's focus, one session at a time, in a cozy little room.

■ A friend who focuses with you
· Your red panda writes, thinks and turns pages at the same desk
· Pick the friend you like: penguin, otter, squirrel or fennec fox
· During breaks, your friend sips a cup of tea and rests too

■ A simple focus timer
· Choose 25 or 50 minutes and start right away
· Short on time? Try a 5 or 15 minute focus
· Pause and resume anytime; the timer keeps going even if you close the app
· Continue into a 5 minute break when a focus ends
· Completion alerts and an option to keep the screen on

■ A room that changes from morning to night
· The window and the light in the room follow your local time: morning, daytime, evening and night

■ Focus sounds
· Rain outside, forest breeze and a warm fire
· Plays without an internet connection

■ Records that add up, little by little
· Every focus day gets a friend stamp on your calendar
· Weekly bars show your focus time by day and the time of day you focus best
· Monthly view shows focus days, daily average and your longest streak
· Set a goal and a D-day, and write yourself a note of encouragement

■ Small gifts for your focus
· As your focus time grows, a plant, a mug and a lamp appear in your room

■ An app you can relax with
· No ads, no sign-up
· Your records stay on your device and are never sent anywhere
· Available in English, 한국어, 日本語 and Español
