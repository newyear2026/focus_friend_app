Use case: ui-mockup.
Design direction name: A desk by the window.
Create one realistic, production-quality Android mobile app startup/loading screen for Deskmate, a cozy focus timer companion app. Redesign the provided existing loading screen radically in composition while maintaining the product's warm illustration identity. Image 1 is the existing app screen and typography/palette reference. Image 2 is the identity and rendering-style reference for the orange red panda, cream cheeks, brown paws, teal overalls and golden desk lamp.
Target dimensions: 390 x 844 logical mobile viewport, rendered at 780 x 1688 or the closest tall portrait ratio. App content only, edge to edge. No device bezel, phone, rounded device mask, notch, OS status bar, clock, signal/battery, home indicator, browser chrome or outer presentation background.
Layout direction: an immersive full-screen illustrated room, NOT an icon in a rounded square. The warm wooden desk and cute red panda occupy the lower middle of the frame. A tall arched window and a softly lit room extend to the top; charcoal plum shadows and warm amber lamplight give a cozy late-evening mood. Outside the window is a quiet muted blue evening sky. The panda is opening a notebook and getting ready to study, comfortably seated, looking gently toward the book. Use the exact recognizable character identity and clean polished 2D cel illustration from image 2, not furry realistic rendering and not 3D. Large forms, simplified background, no visual clutter.
Hierarchy: Place a cream "Deskmate" brand wordmark at about 17% height, centered, 34px logical size, set against a naturally quiet dark upper room area so it is exceptionally readable. Artwork is the hero with generous breathing room around the wordmark. At the bottom, a seamless subtle dark plum gradient over the illustration creates legibility without any panel or card. Place the one-line Korean loading status at about 85% height in 15px warm cream sans serif; below it a slim 120px amber indeterminate loading bar. No percent. Character and desk must remain clearly visible above this text.
Exact visible text only: "Deskmate" and "집중할 공간을 준비하고 있어요."
Palette grounded in the app: plum charcoal #292630, cream #FFEBC5, warm amber #FFC16F, teal from the panda's overalls. Pretendard-like sans serif, accurate Korean, no more than two fonts. Strong hierarchy, purposeful spacing, realistic readable UI typography. No cards, buttons, navigation, metrics, timers, badges, ads or invented features. No extra slogans, no concept labels, no watermarks. One independent screen, never a collage.
