Use case: ui-mockup.
Design direction name: A little reading companion.
Create one realistic, production-quality mobile startup/loading screen concept for Deskmate, a cozy focus timer app with a red panda companion. This is a visual redesign of the existing app loading screenshot (reference image 1). Reference image 2 defines the exact red panda character identity and clean illustrated rendering style: orange fur, cream cheek patches, brown paws, large brown eyes, striped tail, teal overalls. Keep that identity and warm illustration style.
Target dimensions: 390 x 844 logical mobile screen, rendered at 780 x 1688 pixels or closest tall portrait ratio. Output app content only edge-to-edge. No device bezel, phone body, rounded device mask, notch, OS status bar, clock, battery, home indicator, browser chrome, outside design-board background or option label.
Concept: a beautifully calm, spacious, lighter member of the SAME warm Deskmate palette. Use the brand's cream paper #FFF3DE as the continuous background, near-imperceptible paper texture, warm dark plum-brown #50433E typography, teal from the overalls as the small loading accent, soft apricot highlights. Not stark white. It should feel like the quiet opening page of a much-loved storybook. This is a loading screen, not onboarding.
Distinct hierarchy: a small refined 25px semibold "Deskmate" wordmark centered near the top at 22% screen height, with wide breathing room below. Around the middle at 48% height, a compact free-standing character vignette about 185px wide: the recognizable red panda sits beside a closed teal clothbound book, hugging the book with both brown paws and resting its cheek softly against it; striped tail curls around the book. Warm happy anticipation, no sleep symbols. One subtle contact shadow under the book. No circular background, icon tile, frame, surrounding room, window or enclosing card. This vignette is crisp friendly cel illustration, not furry photorealism, not 3D and not watercolor. Character fully visible with natural paws and anatomy.
Below the vignette, at roughly 66% height, a single line of clear 15px Korean status text in soft brown. Under it three tiny well-spaced round teal loading dots, the middle one slightly stronger to suggest a quiet breathing loading animation in a still frame. No loading percentage. Make the generous negative space intentional and elegant.
Visible text exactly and only: "Deskmate" and "집중할 공간을 준비하고 있어요."
Use a Pretendard-like warm sans serif and accurate Korean glyphs. Strong hierarchy, readable type, purposeful spacing. Do not add any other headings, slogans, descriptions, buttons, tap actions, nav, timers, badges, metrics, ads or watermarks. One independent screen only, not a collage. Do not reuse the icon-centered layout from reference 1; use it only as existing product context.
