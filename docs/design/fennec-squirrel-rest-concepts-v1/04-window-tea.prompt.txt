Medium-close views, heads/ears, both arms, chest and cup clearly visible; do not crop tall fennec ears. Same camera, character scale, fixed desk and background within each row.
Style: preserve the references' warm hand-painted 2D animation illustration, delicate warm brown outlines, textured fur, soft light, expressive rounded faces, charming but anatomically coherent paws and connected shoulders.

Motion key poses LEFT TO RIGHT in EACH row:
1. Already turned slightly toward the window on the character's right, gentle three-quarter face still large and clearly visible. BOTH paws cradle the SINGLE mug at mid-chest height. Eyes visibly look toward the distant view, not at the camera. Quiet shoulders, comfortable seated posture.
2. Preserve EXACTLY this window-facing head direction while BOTH paws gently lift the same mug to the mouth. A slow sip while still facing the scenery; eyes softly half-lidded, nose silhouette and spectacles coherent. Closed notebook and laid-down pencil stay motionless on the desk throughout. Keep the full body seated and almost still, never rotate the chair or room. No moving furniture, detached hands, extra arms, duplicate cups or ghost silhouettes. No extra characters, birds, insects, phones or decorations.
